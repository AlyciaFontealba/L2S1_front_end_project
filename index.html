The CPU

The CPU, or central processing unit, is the brain of the computer. Every time you click a button, open a file, or load a webpage, it carries out the instructions behind that action, working through billions of steps every second.

Source: https://computerinfobits.com/learn/parts-of-a-computer-and-their-functions (page no longer available)

The GPU

The GPU, or graphics processing unit, draws everything you see on the screen, from desktop windows to videos and 3D games. A CPU works on a few tasks at a time, while a GPU can handle thousands of tasks at once, which is exactly what drawing an image needs.

Source: https://computerinfobits.com/learn/parts-of-a-computer-and-their-functions (page no longer available)

The Motherboard

The motherboard is the main circuit board inside your computer. Every other part either plugs into it or connects to it, and its job is to hold those parts together and move data and power between them. It also stores the BIOS or UEFI firmware that starts the computer before the operating system loads.

Source: https://computerinfobits.com/learn/parts-of-a-computer-and-their-functions (page no longer available)

The RAM

RAM, or random access memory, is the fast short-term memory your computer uses for whatever you are doing right now. When you open a browser, start a game, or edit a document, the data in use is kept in RAM. RAM is volatile, which means everything in it is wiped when the computer turns off.
